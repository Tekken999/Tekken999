How to set up your phone

Face-on (best for sway, weight shift, arms)

Phone about 3–4 steps in front of you, facing your chest, at hip height. Lean it on your bag or a water bottle.

Down-the-line (best for posture)

Phone 3–4 steps behind you, looking down your target line, at hip height.

  • Your whole body, head to feet, must be in view.
  • Hit Record, walk to the ball, set up and swing.
  • Hold your finish for 2 seconds.
  • Good light helps. Don't stand with the sun right behind you.
